# Business Domain Pack

Use for business models, monetization, market entry, scaling, operations, and commercial strategy.

## Business reality map

Check only the dimensions that affect the decision:

1. **Customer** — who has the problem?
2. **Pain / job** — what outcome do they pay to achieve?
3. **Demand evidence** — behavior, not compliments.
4. **Willingness to pay** — price sensitivity and alternatives.
5. **Acquisition** — channels, CAC, sales cycle.
6. **Conversion** — funnel and friction.
7. **Retention / repeat** — why customers stay or return.
8. **Unit economics** — gross margin, contribution, CAC payback, LTV.
9. **Operations** — capacity, quality, delivery, support.
10. **Scale** — what breaks when volume grows?
11. **Competition** — substitute, incumbent reaction, ease of copying.
12. **Moat / advantage** — data, distribution, switching cost, brand, network, cost, speed.
13. **Regulation / platform dependence** — constraints outside the business.
14. **Cash / runway** — survival under downside.

## Default business route

New idea:
Reframe → demand evidence → base rate → economics → key assumptions → MVA.

Scaling:
Bottleneck → unit economics → second-order effects → capacity → kill/scale criteria.

Declining business:
Issue tree → customer/funnel/economics branches → constraint → test.

## Anti-patterns

- treating TAM as demand;
- treating engagement as willingness to pay;
- scaling before repeatability;
- ignoring distribution;
- using revenue without margin/cash context;
- calling a feature a moat.

## Business output

Prefer:

- business thesis;
- evidence;
- economic engine;
- bottleneck;
- risk;
- next commercial test.
