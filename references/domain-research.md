# Research Domain Pack

Use for deep research, investigations, comparative analysis, evidence synthesis, and questions where establishing truth is central.

## Research frame

1. **Question** — decision-oriented when possible.
2. **Scope** — time, geography, entities, definitions.
3. **Claims** — what must be established?
4. **Source hierarchy** — primary > authoritative secondary > expert > community where appropriate.
5. **Freshness** — current vs evergreen.
6. **Competing hypotheses**
7. **Contradictory evidence**
8. **Unknowns / gaps**
9. **Confidence**
10. **Decision implication**

## Search strategy

Start broad only enough to map the space.
Then search specifically for:

- primary source;
- disconfirming evidence;
- current status;
- exact numbers/definitions;
- opposing interpretations.

Stop when sources become repetitive and the decision is stable.

## Anti-patterns

- source count as a substitute for source quality;
- citing secondary summaries when primary evidence is available;
- ignoring publication/event date;
- treating absence of evidence as evidence of absence;
- overclaiming from one source;
- collecting facts with no decision purpose.

## Output

- answer first;
- evidence by claim;
- disagreements;
- confidence/gaps;
- implication / next research only if decision-relevant.
