# Domain Router

Load a domain pack only when the task clearly belongs to that field and domain-specific checks can materially improve the answer.

Available v1.0 packs:

- [Business](domain-business.md)
- [Investment](domain-investment.md)
- [Product](domain-product.md)
- [Code](domain-code.md)
- [Content](domain-content.md)
- [Research](domain-research.md)

Do not load a pack simply because one domain word appears incidentally.

## Multi-domain tasks

Use at most two domain packs at once unless the task is explicitly cross-functional.

Examples:

- SaaS product strategy → Business + Product.
- Build-vs-buy for an internal platform → Code + Business.
- Content business monetization → Content + Business.
- Public-company deep research → Investment + Research.

Domain packs add **what to inspect**. The core Thinking OS still decides **how deeply to reason**.
